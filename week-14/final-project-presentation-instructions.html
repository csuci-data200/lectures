Data 200: Final Project Presentation Instructions

Fall 2025

Presentation details

  • Your presentation should be 5-7 minutes long
  • All group members should contribute substantially to creating the slides and presenting
  • All data cleaning and analysis should be in the presentation.qmd document, but not all/any of it should be visible in the slideshow
  • Use inline R code when reporting values, instead of just printing output

Peer Evaluation

You will present your projects during our final time. Each of you (as an individual) will evaluate other teams’ presentations using the following rubric.

Category Descriptor Scale
Content Are the research question(s) well designed and is the data being used relevant to the research question(s)? 0 or 1
Content Did the team use appropriate statistical procedures and interpretations of results accurately? 0 or 1
Creativity and Critical Thought Is the project carefully thought out? Are the limitations carefully considered? Does it appear that time and effort went into the planning and implementation of the project? 0 or 1
Slides Are the slides well organized, readable, not full of text, featuring figures with legible labels, legends, etc.? 0 or 1
Professionalism How well did the team present? Does the presentation appear to be well practiced? Are they reading off of a script? Did everyone get a chance to say something meaningful about the project? 0 or 1
Comments Summarize your understanding of the project and provide constructive feedback to the team.

If I detect that you are only assigning full points to everyone in the class then I reserve the right eliminate your evaluation unless I agree that everyone in the class has given a perfect presentation.
